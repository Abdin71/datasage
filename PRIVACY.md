# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 19, 2025

## 1. Introduction

DataSage ("we", "the software") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how DataSage handles data.

## 2. Data Collection

DataSage does NOT collect, transmit, or store your personal information on external servers.

### Local Data Processing

- All automation and data extraction occurs locally on your machine
- Data is processed by your local backend server (localhost:3001)
- No analytics or tracking mechanisms are implemented

### Chrome Storage

DataSage uses Chrome's local storage API to store:

- Automation project configurations (URL, selectors, XPath)
- Session state (last automation status and results)
- User preferences and settings

This data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally.

## 3. Third-Party Access

DataSage does NOT:

- Send data to third-party services
- Use external analytics tools
- Share information with advertisers
- Track user behavior

## 4. Website Interaction

When you run an automation:

- DataSage's backend connects to target websites on your behalf
- Requests originate from your IP address
- Target websites may log your access according to their policies
- You are subject to the target website's privacy policy and terms

## 5. Credentials and Authentication

If you configure authentication:

- Credentials are stored locally in Chrome storage
- Credentials are transmitted only to your local backend server
- No credentials are sent to external services
- You are responsible for securing your device

## 6. Data Security

While DataSage processes data locally, security depends on:

- Your device's security measures
- Your local network configuration
- Access controls on your machine

## 7. Data Retention

Data persists in Chrome storage until:

- You manually delete projects or results
- You clear browser data
- You uninstall the extension

## 8. Children's Privacy

DataSage is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## 10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your stored data via Chrome's storage inspector
- Delete your data by clearing browser data or uninstalling the extension
- Stop using DataSage at any time

## 11. Contact

For privacy concerns, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.

---

**Summary:** DataSage operates entirely locally. Your data never leaves your device. All processing occurs on your machine via the local backend server.
